# Exercise for obesity · Evidence Stability Check v0.1

**Status:** Frozen v1.0 · August 13, 2026  
**Type:** Evidence Stability Check (internal peer review)  
**Artifact:** [`./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md`](./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md) (**Frozen v1.0**)  
**Object:** Exercise training modes for adults with overweight or obesity (aerobic, resistance, combined, HIIT as internal branches)  
**First conclusion under review:** Exercise earns an all-mode composition and fitness seat (C2) in which aerobic training is preferentially advised for modest weight and fat loss averaging not more than 2–3 kg, resistance training for lean-mass preservation during weight loss, and any mode for fitness and insulin sensitivity, so modest mean weight is not “exercise does not matter.”

**Accepted evidence used:** C2 Oppert 2021 EASO Physical Activity Working Group synthesis; A1 AACE 2025; A3 NICE NG246. **Working companion (not seated):** Bellicha et al., 2021 *Obes Rev* (overview of 12 systematic reviews / 149 studies). No sources outside the accepted obesity library inform the seated warrant.

---

## 1. Object definition

| Candidate unit | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Structured exercise training for weight, composition, fitness, and related risk factors | Core object |
| Aerobic; resistance; combined; HIIT | Named internal branches |
| Modest weight vs lean-mass preservation vs fitness | Mandatory job split |
| Exercise as hard-outcome CVD prevention ranking | Not earned from C2 |
| Exercise as an incretin-rival weight ranking | Not earned |
| Eating patterns / behavioural programmes | Outside |

**Combine risk:** “Exercise more” without mode honesty, or crowning HIIT as the only real exercise.  
**Split risk:** One public object per mode before Phase 3 needs it.  
**Inflation risk:** 2–3 kg averages becoming shot-rival weight claims; fitness benefits becoming SELECT-class events.  
**Unit the evidence suggests:** One **all-mode composition/fitness** exercise object with modes internal.

---

## 2. Confidence

| Claim | Confidence | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Aerobic training at moderate intensity is preferentially advised for loss of body weight, total fat, visceral fat, and intra-hepatic fat | High as C2 Grade A direction | Expected weight loss on average not more than 2–3 kg |
| Resistance training at moderate-to-high intensity is advised for preservation of lean mass during weight loss | High as C2 Grade A direction | Different job from kilogram loss |
| Any type of training (aerobic, resistance, combined) or HIIT can be advised for insulin sensitivity and cardiorespiratory fitness | High as C2 Grade A direction | HIIT after CV-risk assessment and under supervision |
| Mode ranking is hard-outcome ranking | Low, reject | C2 is composition, fitness, BP, insulin sensitivity, quality of life; not MACE |
| Modest mean weight means exercise does not matter | Low, reject | Lean mass, fitness, and fat distribution are the object’s other jobs |
| Exercise matches tirzepatide’s one-year weight effect | Low, reject | Different claim type and magnitude |
| A1/A3 place physical activity in obesity care | High for placement | Placement ≠ C2 event upgrade |

**Disagreement lives in:** How to teach 2–3 kg without sounding dismissive; whether HIIT should be featured as loudly as combined training given supervision caveats; and how to keep resistance visible when the public question is “how much will I lose.”

## 3. Alternative interpretations

**A. "2–3 kg is trivial, so demote exercise off the map."**  
Wrong job. C2’s Grade A claims include fat, lean mass, fitness, and insulin sensitivity. Step 0 put exercise on the map.

**B. "Combined or HIIT won some rankings, so delete aerobic."**  
C2 still preferentially advises aerobic for weight and fat. Modes coexist.

**C. "Bellicha’s 149 studies should be seated as C3."**  
Companion overview. Oppert is the recommendation synthesis. Membership not required to calibrate.

**D. "Merge with behavioural programmes or eating patterns as ‘lifestyle.’"**  
Object index separates the three decisions.

---

## 5. Magnitude

C2 (Oppert 2021): synthesis of seven EASO systematic reviews with Grade A recommendations. Aerobic moderate intensity preferentially for weight, total fat, visceral fat, intra-hepatic fat, and blood pressure; expected weight loss on average not more than 2–3 kg. Resistance moderate-to-high intensity for lean-mass preservation during weight loss. Any mode or HIIT (after assessment, supervised) for insulin sensitivity and cardiorespiratory fitness. Resistance alone or combined for muscular fitness.

Bellicha 2021 (companion): 12 SRs, 149 unique studies. Confirms modest weight, clearer fat and composition effects, almost no dedicated weight-maintenance trial base.

That magnitude supports **encourage-without-oversell**, not a kilogram contest with B1 medicines and not omission.

## 6. Hidden assumptions

| Assumption | Risk | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Kilograms are the only job | Job leak | Lean mass, fitness, visceral/hepatic fat |
| Modest mean equals no decision | Charter leak | People still ask what kind of training |
| Fitness improvement is MACE | Surrogate leak | C2 ≠ D1 |
| Unsupervised HIIT inherits supervised HIIT | Safety leak | C2’s assessment/supervision caveat |

---

## 7. Boundary conditions

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults with overweight or obesity considering structured exercise, including people already losing weight by diet or medicines who care about lean mass.

**Does not earn:** Hard-outcome ranking; incretin-rival weight claims; mode-by-mode public architecture from this check alone; merger with eating patterns or behavioural programmes.

**Uncertainty:** Exercise on a GLP-1/tirzepatide background (lean-mass counseling is adjacent, not a seated C); very long-term maintenance trials (Bellicha found almost none).

## 9. Final stress test

1. **Why keep exercise if medicines and surgery lose more weight?**  
   Because C2’s jobs are not B1’s jobs.

2. **Does 2–3 kg make the object a footnote?**  
   No. Composition and fitness are the reason the average is not a dismissal.

3. **Should HIIT become its own object?**  
   No. Internal branch with supervision caveats.

---

## 11. Missing evidence

1. Exercise CVOTs in obesity without diabetes.  
2. Resistance training specifically to preserve lean mass on incretin therapy.  
3. Long-term maintenance RCTs (Bellicha’s almost-empty cell).

**Unresolved before object freeze:** Phase 2 must keep “modest weight” from becoming either a sneer or a silent upgrade into event prevention.

---

## 12. Strongest earned claim

**Exercise earns an all-mode composition and fitness seat (C2): aerobic training is preferentially advised for modest weight and fat loss averaging not more than 2–3 kg, resistance training for lean-mass preservation during weight loss, and any mode for fitness and insulin sensitivity, so modest mean weight is not “exercise does not matter,” mode ranking is not hard-outcome ranking and not an incretin-rival weight ranking, and the library stays at 8.**

## What survives into the artifact

1. Intellectual shape: **all-mode composition/fitness map; modest weight is not irrelevance; not hard events**.  
2. Aerobic vs resistance job split.  
3. Separation from medicines and from behavioural programmes.

**Does not survive:** Exercise-as-shot; exercise-as-failure; HIIT-only public architecture.
