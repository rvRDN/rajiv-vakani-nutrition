## Orientation: three jobs, not one number

C2 is the map. Synthesis of seven EASO systematic reviews with Grade A recommendations.

- **Weight and fat job.** Aerobic training at moderate intensity is preferentially advised for loss of body weight, total fat, visceral fat, and intra-hepatic fat. Expected weight loss on average is not more than 2–3 kg.  
- **Lean-mass job.** Resistance training at moderate-to-high intensity is advised for preservation of lean mass during weight loss. That is a different job from kilogram loss.  
- **Fitness and insulin-sensitivity job.** Any type of training (aerobic, resistance, combined) or HIIT can be advised after cardiovascular-risk assessment and, for HIIT, under supervision.

Those three jobs are why modest mean weight is not irrelevance. Deleting the object because 2–3 kg looks small beside tirzepatide’s −14.9% would be a category error. B1’s job is not C2’s job.

Bellicha 2021 (companion): 12 systematic reviews, 149 unique studies. Confirms modest weight, clearer fat and composition effects, almost no dedicated weight-maintenance trial base. That empty cell is humility, not a reason to unsit the object.

Two collapse moves fail:

1. **Sneer.** “2–3 kg, so exercise does not matter.”  
2. **Upgrade.** Fitness or blood-pressure improvement becoming SELECT-class events, or combined/HIIT becoming the only real exercise.

Modes stay internal. Walking is not obsolete because HIIT exists. Unsupervised HIIT does not inherit supervised HIIT.

A1 and A3 place physical activity in obesity care. Placement is not an event upgrade.

## Boundaries to carry forward

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults comparing exercise modes for weight, composition, and fitness, including people losing weight by other means who care about lean mass.

**Does not earn:** Hard-outcome ranking; incretin-rival weight claims; mode-by-mode public architecture from this artifact; merger with eating patterns or behavioural programmes.

**Keep uncertain:** Exercise on a GLP-1/tirzepatide background (lean-mass counseling is adjacent); very long-term maintenance RCTs; how loudly HIIT should be featured given supervision caveats.

---

## Bottom line for later synthesis

1. Keep the all-mode composition/fitness map.  
2. Keep the three jobs: aerobic modest weight/fat; resistance lean mass during weight loss; any mode for fitness and insulin sensitivity.  
3. Keep “modest weight is not irrelevance.” Do not sneer and do not upgrade.  
4. Keep ranking as composition/fitness, not events and not an incretin-rival weight ranking.  
5. Keep modes internal.  
6. Keep this object separate from behavioural programmes and from medicines.
