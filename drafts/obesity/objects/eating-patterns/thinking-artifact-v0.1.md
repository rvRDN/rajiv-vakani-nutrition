**Working question:** How do you keep a crowded pattern map when six-month named-diet differences are small, 12-month fade is the grain, and a formula-diet branch exists without becoming the type 2 diabetes remission object?

---

## Orientation: fade is part of the claim

C1 is the map. One hundred twenty-one trials; 21,942 people; 14 named programmes. Versus usual diet at six months, low-carbohydrate and low-fat are similar (4.63 vs 4.37 kg). Named-diet examples: Atkins 5.5 kg, Zone 4.1 kg, DASH 3.6 kg. Between-diet gaps are smaller than diet versus no diet. Weight loss diminished at 12 months. Risk-factor benefits largely disappeared except Mediterranean.

That remainder is still a risk-factor remainder. It is not MI, stroke, or death. Pattern ranking is not hard-outcome ranking.

Vegetarian and Ornish-class programmes are in the network. Presence is honesty. It is not a device for manufacturing unstudied vegan-only plates from Mediterranean evidence.

Two collapse moves fail, and they fail in opposite directions:

1. **Championship.** Treating Atkins’s six-month lead as the obesity diet.  
2. **Deletion.** Treating 12-month fade as proof that diets do not work, so only shots and surgery remain. Fade is what the evidence earned. People still choose among patterns.

---

## VLCD stays a branch

Parretti 2016 (working, not seated): VLED plus a behavioural programme versus a behavioural programme alone, −3.9 kg (−6.7 to −1.1) at one year; −1.4 kg at 24 months; −1.3 kg at 38–60 months (CI includes little or no difference at the longest follow-up). Premature discontinuation similar (19% vs 20%).

That calibrates the formula-diet branch A3 places. It does not become DiRECT. Different campaign, eligibility, and remission job. It does not become a seventh object. Ge’s named-programme network is not a formula-diet protocol, which is why the branch exists inside this object rather than being assumed from C1.

Meal-replacement *as a programme component* lives on behavioural programmes. Diet-quality VLCD lives here. Do not let the component swallow the branch, or the branch swallow the delivery object.

## Boundaries to carry forward

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults comparing studied eating patterns, or considering a supervised formula/VLCD protocol, with honesty about 12-month fade.

**Does not earn:** Hard-event pattern ranking; vegan=Mediterranean transfer; DiRECT import; VLCD as a seventh object; merger with behavioural programmes or exercise.

**Keep uncertain:** Trials after C1’s 2018 search cutoff; commercial formula products versus trial VLEDs; how loudly Atkins’s six-month lead may speak given small between-diet gaps.

---

## Bottom line for later synthesis

1. Keep the pattern-level weight/risk-factor map. Low-carbohydrate and low-fat similar at six months; named-diet differences small.  
2. Keep 6- vs 12-month fade attached to every rank. Fade is not deletion.  
3. Keep weight/risk factors ≠ events.  
4. Keep vegetarian on the map without inventing vegan-only claims.  
5. Keep VLCD as a mandatory internal branch with working evidence. Do not import DiRECT. Do not split from this artifact.  
6. Keep this object separate from behavioural programmes and exercise.

> Eating patterns earn a pattern-level weight and risk-factor seat (C1): low-carbohydrate and low-fat are similar at six months, named-diet differences are small, weight loss diminished at 12 months, vegetarian/Ornish-class programmes are on the map rather than invented from Mediterranean evidence, that ranking is not hard-outcome ranking, VLCD/formula diets stay a mandatory internal branch calibrated with working evidence (about 4 kg extra at one year vs a behavioural programme alone, smaller later) without becoming DiRECT or a seventh object, and the library stays at 8.
