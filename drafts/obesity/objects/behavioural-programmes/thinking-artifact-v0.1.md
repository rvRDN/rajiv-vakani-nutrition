## Orientation: both halves of the durability finding

This object exists because people choose programmes, and A3 places behavioural services in the UK pathway. It does not exist because a permanent domain C was earned. Membership gate closed. Library stays at 8. That is a successful calibration, not an incomplete one.

Hartmann-Boyce 2021 (working): 249 trials; 59,081 people; mean follow-up about two years. After programme end, intervention groups regained faster than controls (0.12–0.32 kg/year). The between-group difference was maintained for at least five years. Each kilogram lost at programme end was associated with faster later regain (0.13–0.19 kg/year).

Those sentences must travel together. “Faster regain” alone becomes “programmes fail.” “Maintained difference” alone becomes a quieter miracle than the evidence earned. The owner freeze of §12 locked both halves. Later phases may not pick.

Hartmann-Boyce 2022 (working): among components at 12 months, diet change MD −1.84 kg; partial meal replacement −2.12; total meal replacement −2.63; psychologist/counselor −1.45; dietitian −1.31; home setting −1.05. Component signals are not a reason to merge this object with eating patterns. Pattern quality is still a different decision from programme delivery. Meal replacement *as a component* here must not swallow the VLCD *diet-quality branch* there.

Madigan (working): primary-care packages about −2.3 kg (−3.0 to −1.6) at 12 months vs comparator. Report the number. Do not convert it into events. Do not convert it into deletion beside incretins.

Named commercial programmes do not earn distinct promotion beyond the shelf. DiRECT stays on the type 2 diabetes map.

Two collapse moves fail, and they fail in opposite directions:

1. **Silent seating.** Treating usable working syntheses as automatic permanent Cs.  
2. **Omission.** Dropping the object because it failed the library test. “No seated C” is allowed. Deletion-for-no-C is not.

A later null or insufficient finding remains permitted if Phase 3 cannot convert this grain into a stable landscape claim. That permission is not a present deletion order.

## Boundaries to carry forward

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults comparing a structured behavioural programme with unsupervised diet change or with other on-map options.

**Does not earn:** Seated C membership; hard-outcome prevention; named-brand promotion; merger with eating patterns or exercise; DiRECT import.

**Keep uncertain:** Programme effects on a modern incretin background; how loudly a 2 kg primary-care average should speak; whether any named commercial programme could later earn distinct placement (not this Phase 1).

## Bottom line for later synthesis

1. Investigated delivery object; membership gate closed; library stays at 8.  
2. Keep regain after programme end *and* the maintained between-group difference as one grain.  
3. Weight-change evidence only. Not a SELECT-class event warrant.  
4. Keep named commercial programmes unpromoted.  
5. Keep this object separate from eating patterns and exercise. Meal-replacement-as-component must not swallow VLCD-as-branch.  
6. Do not dress this object up as a missing-C problem, and do not omit it for neatness.
