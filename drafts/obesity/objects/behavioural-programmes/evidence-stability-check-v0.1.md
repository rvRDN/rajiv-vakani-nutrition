# Behavioural weight-management programmes · Evidence Stability Check v0.1

**Status:** Frozen v1.0 · August 13, 2026  
**Type:** Evidence Stability Check (internal peer review)  
**Artifact:** [`./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md`](./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md) (**Frozen v1.0**)  
**Object:** Structured behavioural weight-management programmes as a delivery decision (not eating-pattern quality; not unstructured “try harder”)  
**First conclusion under review:** Behavioural programmes survive as an investigated delivery object with no seated C and no membership proposal: A3 supplies pathway placement, labeled working evidence can calibrate magnitude and durability, and a null or insufficient finding remains permitted.

**Accepted evidence used:** A3 NICE NG246 (only seated source with a behavioural-services chapter). A1/A2 supply lifestyle-conjunction placement, not a behavioural NMA. **Working evidence (not seated):** Hartmann-Boyce et al. 2021 *BMJ* n1840 (long-term weight after behavioural programmes); Hartmann-Boyce et al. 2022 *Obesity* 30(9):1778–1786 (programme components); Madigan et al. primary-care package magnitude (about −2.3 kg at 12 months vs comparator). Membership gate: do not open a Phase 0 membership proposal unless this check cannot calibrate without a seated C.

## 3. Alternative interpretations

**A. "No seated C, so delete the object."**  
That confuses library membership with object existence. Step 0 and the accepted index kept the decision because people face it. A3 places it. Working evidence can calibrate it.

**B. "Seat Hartmann-Boyce 2021 as C4 so the object looks like the others."**  
Membership inflation. Phase 0 already judged there was no permanent domain-level C. This check can calibrate without reopening the library.

**C. "Faster regain after programme end means programmes fail."**  
Hartmann-Boyce 2021’s grain is both: faster regain *and* a maintained between-group difference for at least five years.

**D. "Merge into eating patterns because diet change is the biggest component."**  
Component signal ≠ object identity. People still choose a programme vs a pattern vs unsupervised diet change.

**E. "This is DiRECT / formula-diet remission."**  
Campaign leak. VLCD lives as an eating-patterns branch. DiRECT stays on the type 2 diabetes map.

## 5. Magnitude

A3 places behavioural weight-management services in the UK pathway (adults; children also in NG246 but this object’s first grain is adult programme delivery unless Phase 2 later needs a pediatric rail).

Hartmann-Boyce 2021 (working): 249 RCTs; 59,081 participants; mean follow-up about two years. After programme end, intervention groups regained faster than controls, but the between-group difference was maintained for at least five years. Larger end-of-programme loss predicted faster later regain.

Hartmann-Boyce 2022 (working): among programme components, diet change and meal-replacement strategies had the larger 12-month signals; specialist delivery (psychologist/counselor, dietitian) and home setting also associated with greater loss.

Madigan primary-care MA (working): about −2.3 kg at 12 months vs comparator.

That magnitude supports **a modest, durable-difference delivery claim with honest regain**, not a SELECT-class event warrant, not a commercial-brand championship, and not a reason to unsit the object.

## 9. Final stress test

1. **Why keep an object Phase 0 did not seat a C for?**  
   Because people still choose programmes, A3 places them, and working evidence can say what they earn.

2. **Why not seat Hartmann-Boyce to look consistent with medicines?**  
   Consistency-of-furniture is not a membership test. Phase 0 already ran that test.

3. **Does 2 kg at 12 months make this a footnote to incretins?**  
   Different decision. Medicines do not erase programme delivery.

4. **Does faster regain kill the object?**  
   No. The maintained between-group difference is the other half of the same finding.

## 11. Missing evidence

1. A living GRADE network of programme *delivery* (not diet quality) that Phase 0 judged was not a permanent domain C.  
2. Hard-outcome trials of behavioural programmes in obesity without diabetes.  
3. Programme effects on a modern incretin background.

**Unresolved before object freeze:** Phase 2 must keep regain and maintained difference in the same breath, and must keep meal-replacement-as-component from swallowing the VLCD branch on eating patterns.
