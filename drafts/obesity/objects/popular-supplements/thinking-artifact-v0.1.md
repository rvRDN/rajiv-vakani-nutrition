## Orientation: investigated, not promoted

This object exists because people are sold green tea, Garcinia, CLA, chromium, and neighbors for weight loss, not because a permanent C was earned. Membership gate closed. Library stays at 8. That is a successful calibration, not an incomplete one.

A1–A3 supply placement honesty. Silence and caution are part of the map. They do not make named bottles core replacements for medicines, patterns, programmes, or procedures.

Labeled working evidence, kept as branches:

- **Green tea:** Jurgens Cochrane. Small, not clinically important. No maintenance benefit.  
- **Garcinia and other single-agent herbals:** Maunder 2020. No clinically significant (≥2.5 kg) single-agent effect for Garcinia or green tea.  
- **Phaseolus:** Statistically significant in that package, below the clinical bar. Not a promotion.  
- **CLA, chromium, Ayurvedic products that surface:** Named shelf items to be checked against the same bar, not promoted in advance. Ayurvedic products stay named branches. Do not import a prior Insights claim ladder.

Two collapse moves fail, and they fail in opposite directions:

1. **Silent seating / promotion.** Treating usable working reviews as automatic permanent Cs, or treating popularity as the warrant. Green tea is plant-based marketing, not a transferred medicines-class claim.  
2. **Omission.** Dropping the shelf because it failed the library test. “Small / not clinically important / insufficient” is earned after looking. A faithful map of a crowded market includes what was studied and did not earn much.

A later null or insufficient finding remains permitted. Currently that direction is more honest than a positive medicines-class warrant. Permission to freeze a small/insufficient claim is not a present deletion order.

Do not invent five supplement objects. Do not invent a representative-supplement C for aisle completeness.
