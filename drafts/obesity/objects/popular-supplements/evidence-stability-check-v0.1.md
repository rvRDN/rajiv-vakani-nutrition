# Popular supplements for obesity · Evidence Stability Check v0.1

**Status:** Frozen v1.0 · August 13, 2026  
**Type:** Evidence Stability Check (internal peer review)  
**Artifact:** [`./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md`](./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md) (**Frozen v1.0**)  
**Object:** Popular over-the-counter supplements and herbal products people actually buy for weight loss  
**First conclusion under review:** Popular supplements survive as an investigated popular market with no seated C and no membership proposal: A-set honesty plus labeled working evidence, a null or insufficient finding is permitted, and promotion requires evidence rather than popularity.

**Accepted evidence used:** A1, A2, A3 for placement honesty only (what guidelines do or do not do with this market). **No seated C.** **Working evidence (not seated):** Jurgens et al. Cochrane green tea (CD008650); Maunder et al. 2020 *Diabetes Obes Metab* herbal meta-analysis. Membership gate: do not open a Phase 0 membership proposal unless this check cannot calibrate without a seated C. Promotion gate: no named product earns distinct promotion beyond the shelf.

## 1. Object definition

| Candidate unit | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Popular OTC supplements and herbals sold for weight loss (green tea, Garcinia, CLA, chromium, Ayurvedic products that surface) | Core object (investigated market) |
| Named products as they appear in working evidence | Named internal branches / shelf items |
| A-set guideline silence or caution | Placement honesty, not a C-class warrant |
| Seated domain C for supplements | None. Phase 0 found no permanent C |
| Import of Insights / Ayurvedic investigation architecture | Outside |
| Upgrade of a small analogue or single-agent signal into a medicines-class seat | Not earned |

**Combine risk:** Dumping the market into “lifestyle” so the page never says what people actually buy.  
**Split risk:** One public object per product, or an imported Ayurvedic ladder.  
**Inflation risk:** Popularity as evidence; statistically significant grams as clinically important weight loss; plant-based marketing as transferred fish-oil/medicine logic (not the main analogue here, but the honesty rule still applies: do not invent unstudied products).  
**Unit the evidence suggests:** One **investigated popular market** that can stay on the map even if the earned claim is small, insufficient, or null.

---

## 3. Alternative interpretations

**A. "No seated C, so delete the object."**  
Same error as behavioural programmes. Library membership ≠ object existence. The index kept the market because people face it.

**B. "Seat the Cochrane review or Maunder so the object looks like medicines."**  
Membership inflation. Phase 0 already judged there was no permanent domain C. Working evidence can say “small / not clinically important.”

**C. "Green tea is plant-based, so upgrade it as the responsible alternative to medicines."**  
Plant-based analogue honesty runs the other way: do not invent a clinical claim from marketing category. Green tea’s own trials are small.

**D. "Phaseolus was statistically significant, so promote white-kidney-bean extract."**  
Maunder: statistically but not clinically significant. Promotion gate stays closed.

**E. "If the finding is null, we wasted a folder."**  
A faithful map of a crowded market includes “this is what was studied, and it did not earn much.” That is the charter, not a failed object.

**Why A survives:** Index gates block B, C, D, and E. Working evidence is already enough to say the market is mostly small or not clinically important.

**Intellectual shape:** **Investigated popular market; no seated C; small/not clinically important working signals; promotion requires evidence; null permitted.**

---

## 5. Magnitude

A1–A3: placement honesty. They do not seat a supplement NMA. Silence and caution are part of the map.

Jurgens Cochrane (working): green tea preparations; small, not clinically important; no maintenance benefit.

Maunder 2020 (working): herbal single agents; no clinically significant (≥2.5 kg) effect for green tea or Garcinia; *Phaseolus* statistically significant but below that clinical bar.

CLA, chromium, and Ayurvedic products that surface remain named shelf items to be checked in Phase 2 against the same bar, not promoted in advance.

That magnitude supports **an honest popular-market object whose current grain is small or insufficient**, not a medicines-class seat and not deletion.

## 7. Boundary conditions

**Does not earn:** Hard-outcome prevention; named-product promotion from popularity; a seated C from this check; Insights-architecture import; merger into medicines or eating patterns.

## 9. Final stress test

1. **Why keep a folder if the signal is small?**  
   Because the market is crowded and the charter forbids a top-three shortlist that pretends the shelf is empty.

2. **Why not seat Cochrane to look consistent?**  
   Phase 0 already ran membership. Working evidence is enough to calibrate “small.”

3. **Does “not clinically important” mean delete?**  
   No. It may be the earned claim.

4. **Should Ayurveda become its own object because it is culturally distinct?**  
   Not from this check. Named branches if products surface.

## 11. Missing evidence

1. A living GRADE network of popular weight-loss supplements that Phase 0 judged was not a permanent domain C.  
2. Adequate trials of CLA, chromium, and named Ayurvedic products against a clinical-importance bar.  
3. Maintenance and harm packages, not only short-term kilograms.

**Unresolved before object freeze:** Phase 2 must name shelf items without converting investigation into endorsement, and must be willing to freeze a small/insufficient claim if that is what survives.

## 12. Strongest earned claim

**Popular supplements survive as an investigated popular market with no seated C and no membership proposal: A-set honesty plus labeled working evidence shows small or non-significant weight signals (green-tea Cochrane not clinically important; herbal meta-analysis without clinically significant single-agent effects for Garcinia and green tea), no hard-outcome seats, no named product earns distinct promotion beyond the shelf, Ayurvedic products stay named branches rather than an imported Insights ladder, a later null or insufficient finding remains permitted, and the library stays at 8.**
