**Object:** Medicines used in chronic weight management whose decision value may be weight, cardiovascular, or complication-specific  
**Intellectual shape:** **On-map obesity medicines; weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking; SELECT population internal to semaglutide; investigational watches; compounded fence.**

**Accepted evidence used:** B1 Nong 2026 *BMJ* NMA; A1 AACE 2025 algorithm; A2 Obesity Canada 2025 pharmacotherapy update; A3 NICE NG246; D1 SELECT 2023.  
**Stance:** Medicines earn a comparative weight, harm, and selected hard-outcome seat. They do not win a lifestyle-versus-drugs popularity contest, smear SELECT onto tirzepatide or onto everyone with obesity, or import the Emerging Therapies studio as architecture.  
**Job:** Keep B1’s GRADE spine visible while holding the divider that must not be averaged away: one-year weight ranking is not mortality/MI ranking, and SELECT’s enrollment is not the obesity population.

**Evidentiary shape (do not flatten in later phases):** Comparative weight/harm spine plus a population-bound MACE warrant inside one agent. Not a kilogram league table. Not a studio folio. Not the type 2 diabetes medicines object.

## Orientation: two rankings, one object

B1 is the quantitative center. Search to 12 November 2025; 262 RCTs; 99,791 people; 19 drugs; GRADE. At one year versus lifestyle modification: tirzepatide −14.9%; CagriSema −14.8%; oral semaglutide −10.9%; orforglipron −9.9%; subcutaneous semaglutide −9.8%; phentermine-topiramate −8.1% (moderate to high certainty for those six). Discontinuation because of adverse events is highest with orforglipron, naltrexone-bupropion, liraglutide, phentermine-topiramate, CagriSema, and oral semaglutide (RR 1.9 to 4.2). No drug in the package improved quality of life beyond a small MID. Tirzepatide reduced fat mass (−25.7%) and lean mass (−8.3%).

That is an on-map pharmacologic seat. It is not permission to erase lifestyle, behavioural, or procedure objects.

D1 sits inside the semaglutide branch, not beside the object and not over the whole class. SELECT: 17,604 people; age ≥45; preexisting CVD; BMI ≥27; no diabetes; semaglutide 2.4 mg versus placebo; mean follow-up 39.8 months; 3-point MACE 6.5% vs 8.0%; HR 0.80 (0.72–0.90). B1’s all-cause mortality and MI signals for subcutaneous semaglutide are largely CVOT-informed in high-risk populations. They do not travel to tirzepatide’s larger weight effect. They do not travel to primary-prevention obesity. SURMOUNT-MMO is unfinished. A heart-failure signal for tirzepatide is not a SELECT export.

Three controversies stay inside the object rather than becoming three objects:

1. **Weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking.** Tirzepatide and CagriSema lead kilograms. Subcutaneous semaglutide leads the mortality/MI package. Do not average them into one “best shot.”  
2. **SELECT population ≠ everyone with obesity.** Age, established CVD, no diabetes. Internal to semaglutide 2.4 mg.  
3. **A1–A3 culture.** Complication-centric U.S. algorithm vs GRADE long-term pharmacotherapy vs UK pathway. Sequencing disagreement is preserved, not silently won.

Older approved agents remain named branches. Access is part of the desperate-reader test. Investigational neighbors (CagriSema, orforglipron, retatrutide, and others B1 reports at low or mixed certainty) stay watches. Compounded products are A2’s safety fence, not a therapy branch to optimize. Emerging Therapies remains the molecule-level room. SURPASS-CVOT and DiRECT stay on the type 2 diabetes map.

## Boundaries to carry forward

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults whose obesity care includes medicines whose job may be weight, cardiovascular, or complication-specific.

**Does not earn:** SELECT export to everyone with obesity; tirzepatide MACE superiority; investigational-agent promotion; compounded-product protocols; medicines-as-only-real-treatment; type 2 diabetes merge; studio import.

**Keep uncertain:** SURMOUNT-MMO; how loudly to teach tirzepatide’s weight lead without converting it into a CV ranking; A2’s long-term-use stance versus “a course of shots” as the reader’s time horizon; living updates to B1.

---

## Bottom line for later synthesis

1. Keep B1’s comparative one-year weight, harm, and selected-event seat on the map.  
2. Keep weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking as mandatory internal structure.  
3. Keep SELECT population-bound and internal to semaglutide 2.4 mg.  
4. Keep older agents as named branches; investigational neighbors as watches; compounded products as a fence.  
5. Keep A1–A3 culture disagreement visible.  
6. Sit beside lifestyle, behavioural programmes, and procedures; do not replace them.

**Strongest earned claim (carried verbatim from the accepted Stability Check §12):**

> Obesity medicines earn an on-map seat from comparative one-year weight, harm, and selected hard-outcome evidence (B1), while subcutaneous semaglutide uniquely carries a SELECT-informed MACE and all-cause-mortality signal in high-risk populations (D1) that must not be smeared onto tirzepatide’s larger weight effect or onto everyone with obesity, older approved agents remain named branches, investigational neighbors stay low-certainty watches rather than studio-imported objects, compounded products remain a safety fence, A1–A3 disagree in culture without breaking the object, and the library stays at 8.
