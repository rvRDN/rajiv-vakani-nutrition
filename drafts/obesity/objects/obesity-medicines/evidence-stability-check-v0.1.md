# Obesity medicines · Evidence Stability Check v0.1

**Status:** Frozen v1.0 · August 13, 2026  
**Type:** Evidence Stability Check (internal peer review)  
**Artifact:** [`./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md`](./thinking-artifact-v0.1.md) (**Frozen v1.0**)  
**Object:** Medicines used in chronic weight management whose decision value may be weight, cardiovascular, or complication-specific (semaglutide 2.4 mg, tirzepatide, and older approved agents as internal branches; investigational neighbors as watches; compounded products as a safety fence)  
**First conclusion under review:** Obesity medicines earn an on-map seat from comparative one-year weight, harm, and selected hard-outcome evidence (B1), while subcutaneous semaglutide uniquely carries a SELECT-informed MACE and all-cause-mortality signal in high-risk populations that must not be smeared onto tirzepatide’s larger weight effect or onto everyone with obesity.

**Accepted evidence used:** B1 Nong 2026 *BMJ* NMA; A1 AACE 2025 algorithm; A2 Obesity Canada 2025 pharmacotherapy update; A3 NICE NG246; D1 SELECT 2023. No sources outside the accepted obesity library inform the seated warrant. Investigational-agent certainty ratings in B1 are the studio-boundary fence, not an Emerging Therapies import.

## 1. Object definition

| Candidate unit | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Medicines used for chronic weight management (weight, CV, or complication-specific jobs) | Core object |
| Semaglutide 2.4 mg; tirzepatide; liraglutide; phentermine-topiramate; naltrexone-bupropion; orlistat | Named internal branches |
| SELECT MACE in established CVD without diabetes | Internal to the semaglutide branch (D1) |
| CagriSema, orforglipron, retatrutide, and neighbors B1 reports at low or mixed certainty | Named watches, not promotions |
| Compounded / unregulated peptides | Safety fence (A2), not a therapy branch |
| Type 2 diabetes medicines object / SURPASS-CVOT / DiRECT | Outside this map |
| Emerging Therapies studio folios as architecture | Outside |

**Combine risk:** Treating “obesity medicines” as one interchangeable weight-loss slogan that erases the weight-versus-MACE divider, or smearing SELECT onto tirzepatide and onto primary-prevention obesity.  
**Split risk:** One public object per drug, a separate SELECT object, or a diabetes-versus-obesity split of dual agonists.  
**Inflation risk:** One-year weight ranking becoming mortality/MI ranking; low-certainty emerging agents becoming approved-medicine warrants; studio claim ladders becoming this object’s architecture.  
**Unit the evidence suggests:** One **on-map obesity-medicines** object with weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking as mandatory internal structure.

## 3. Alternative interpretations

**A. "Tirzepatide lost more weight, so it is the cardiovascular winner."**  
B1 and D1 exist to stop that inference. Weight ranking ≠ mortality/MI ranking.

**B. "SELECT means every GLP-1 or dual agonist prevents heart attacks in obesity."**  
Population and agent leak. Established CVD, no diabetes, semaglutide 2.4 mg.

**C. "Because A1–A3 disagree, medicines have no stable claim."**  
Too destructive. B1’s weight/harm GRADE spine and D1’s MACE warrant survive across guideline cultures. Sequencing culture stays contested.

**D. "Promote CagriSema or retatrutide because B1’s weight numbers look similar to tirzepatide."**  
Certainty and approval status forbid it. Object-index watches. Emerging Therapies remains the molecule-level room.

**E. "Older agents are obsolete, so drop those branches."**  
B1 still quantifies them. Desperate-reader test includes people who cannot access or tolerate incretins.

**F. "This is the type 2 diabetes medicines object."**  
Overlap is real. Research object is obesity. SURPASS-CVOT and DiRECT stay on that map. Dual agonists remain investigable here for the obesity decision.

## 5. Magnitude

B1 (Nong 2026): search to 12 November 2025; 262 RCTs; 99,791 participants; 19 drugs; GRADE. One-year weight vs lifestyle modification: tirzepatide −14.9%; CagriSema −14.8%; oral semaglutide −10.9%; orforglipron −9.9%; subcutaneous semaglutide −9.8%; phentermine-topiramate −8.1% (moderate to high certainty for those six). Emerging agents (ecnoglutide, mazdutide, retatrutide) 13.1–14.6% at very low to low certainty. Discontinuation because of adverse events highest with orforglipron, naltrexone-bupropion, liraglutide, phentermine-topiramate, CagriSema, and oral semaglutide (RR 1.9 to 4.2). GI events most increased with naltrexone-bupropion, oral semaglutide, orforglipron, and tirzepatide (RR 3.1 to 4.2). Subcutaneous semaglutide uniquely associated with lower all-cause mortality and MI. Semaglutide and tirzepatide reduced heart-failure risk. No convincing kidney-failure reduction. No convincing quality-of-life improvement beyond MID.

D1 (SELECT 2023): 17,604 randomized; mean follow-up 39.8 months; MACE 6.5% vs 8.0%; HR 0.80 (0.72–0.90). Discontinuation for adverse events 16.6% vs 8.2%.

A1–A3 contribute placement rather than a second NMA: U.S. complication-centric medication hierarchies (A1); Canadian GRADE long-term pharmacotherapy, SELECT-aware MACE rec in ASCVD with BMI ≥27, and compounded-product warning (A2); UK medicines-within-pathway placement (A3).

That magnitude supports **class- and agent-level weight and harm claims plus one agent’s high-risk MACE warrant**, not a claim that the biggest weight loss is the best heart protection, not a claim that SELECT covers primary-prevention obesity, and not a claim that investigational agents are interchangeable with labeled medicines.

## 7. Boundary conditions

**Applies most clearly to:** Adults with overweight or obesity considering or already using medicines for chronic weight management, including people with established CVD considering semaglutide 2.4 mg.

**Does not earn:** SELECT export to everyone with obesity; tirzepatide MACE superiority; investigational-agent promotion; compounded-product optimization; replacement of lifestyle, behavioural, or procedure objects; type 2 diabetes merge.

**Guideline boundary:** A1, A2, and A3 disagree in culture. Phase 2/3 must teach the disagreement, not pick a silent winner.

**Uncertainty:** SURMOUNT-MMO; living updates to B1; access, cost, and supply; lean-mass loss counseling; pregnancy and pediatric pathways (watch).

## 9. Final stress test

1. **Why keep older agents if incretins dominate B1’s weight network?**  
   Because people still use them, B1 still quantifies them, and access is part of the desperate-reader test.

2. **Does tirzepatide’s −14.9% force a CV upgrade?**  
   No. That is the object’s central divider.

3. **Does SELECT force a separate cardiovascular-medicines object?**  
   No. Internal to semaglutide. Exporting it would invent a second map.

4. **Does B1’s CagriSema weight force promotion?**  
   No. Watch. Studio remains the molecule-level room.

5. **Should compounded products be investigated as a branch to optimize?**  
   No. A2 fence. Safety context, not a protocol.

## 11. Missing evidence

1. Tirzepatide obesity-without-diabetes MACE (SURMOUNT-MMO).  
2. Longer-than-one-year comparative GRADE for labeled medicines, including after discontinuation.  
3. Quality-of-life instruments that can exceed B1’s MID where patient-important change actually occurs.  
4. Head-to-head labeled-agent CVOTs.

**Unresolved before object freeze:** Phase 2 must keep “weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking” from collapsing into either a kilogram league table or a claim that only SELECT-eligible people have a medicines decision.

## 12. Strongest earned claim

**Obesity medicines earn an on-map seat from comparative one-year weight, harm, and selected hard-outcome evidence (B1), while subcutaneous semaglutide uniquely carries a SELECT-informed MACE and all-cause-mortality signal in high-risk populations (D1) that must not be smeared onto tirzepatide’s larger weight effect or onto everyone with obesity, older approved agents remain named branches, investigational neighbors stay low-certainty watches rather than studio-imported objects, compounded products remain a safety fence, A1–A3 disagree in culture without breaking the object, and the library stays at 8.**

1. Intellectual shape: **on-map obesity medicines; weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking; SELECT population internal to semaglutide; investigational watches; compounded fence**.  
2. Named branches with promotion gate closed for investigational agents.  
3. Separation from type 2 diabetes medicines and from Emerging Therapies architecture.
