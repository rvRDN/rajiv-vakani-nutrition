## D. Landmark trials

### D1. Lincoff AM, Brown-Frandsen K, Colhoun HM, et al. Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes in Obesity without Diabetes (SELECT)
- **Journal:** *New England Journal of Medicine*. 2023;389(24):2221–2232
- **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa2307563
- **PMID:** 37952131
- **Primary URL:** https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2307563
- **ClinicalTrials.gov:** NCT03574597
- **Scope note:** 17,604 adults ≥45 years with preexisting CVD, BMI ≥27, and no diabetes. Semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly vs placebo. 3-point MACE HR 0.80 (0.72–0.90). Mean follow-up 39.8 months.
