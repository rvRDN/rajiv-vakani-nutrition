## Methodological preface

This document is not a popularity ranking, an effect-size leaderboard, or a count of positive studies.

**Placement follows the strength and scope of each frozen §12 claim.** Comparative one-year weight/harm plus a population-bound MACE warrant vs long-term procedural weight with an observational mortality companion vs seated pattern and exercise syntheses vs investigated no-C objects all affect placement. Claim type is the first sorting key. Search heat is not.

The public reader problem is broader than any one object. People arrive with “obesity” or “weight loss” and ask:

- Do shots replace diet, exercise, programmes, or surgery?
- Which medicine loses the most weight, and does that mean it protects the heart?
- Does SELECT mean every GLP-1 prevents heart attacks in everyone with obesity?
- Which diet is best, and do the kilos last?
- Are formula / VLCD diets a different decision from “eat this pattern”?
- Does exercise even matter if the average is only a few kilograms?
- Do commercial or NHS programmes work, or does the weight just come back?
- Is endoscopy a gentler surgery, or a different option?
- Do green tea, Garcinia, or “fat burners” replace care?

A faithful map must answer those questions without becoming a recommendation engine. The hub orients. Topic pages teach participation, monitoring, burden, fit, limits, and useful clinician questions.

Unlike hypertension, this ladder has **no parallel hard-outcome product sibling** (no salt-substitute analogue; SELECT is internal to one medicine, not a second Band A decision). Unlike type 2 diabetes, there are **not two remission routes**. Behavioural programmes are not DiRECT. VLCD is not a remission object. Surgery on this map is long-term weight among procedures, not T2D remission versus pharmacologic therapy. The consequential splits here are **weight ranking vs MACE ranking inside medicines**, **surgery vs endoscopy inside procedures**, **pattern quality vs programme delivery vs exercise**, and **6- vs 12-month durability**.

**Landscape teaching principle:** Weight, waist, and composition change are not the same as heart attack, stroke, or death. SELECT-class findings do not automatically transfer to every anti-obesity medicine or to everyone with obesity. Durability and regain are part of the claim. Those distinctions must stay visible on the hub and on every surrogate topic page.

**Shape lock (from Phase 2):** The six objects have genuinely different evidentiary shapes. Phase 3 places them. It does not rewrite them into one “evidence summary” voice.

| Object | Evidentiary shape |
|---|---|
| Obesity medicines | Comparative weight/harm spine plus a population-bound MACE warrant inside one agent |
| Eating patterns | Comparative weight/risk-factor gradient with a mandatory 6- vs 12-month durability split; VLCD internal |
| Exercise | Composition/fitness map with three jobs; modest weight is not irrelevance |
| Behavioural programmes | Delivery object with no seated C; regain *and* maintained between-group difference as one grain |
| Procedures | GRADE network with two non-collapsed branches; short-term incretin annotates; SOS observational, not SELECT-class |
| Popular supplements | Deliberately negative promotion result after looking |

### How to read the letters (internal only)

The letters name **landscape bands** (pharmacologic weight/harm with a population-bound MACE warrant; long-term procedural weight with two branches; pattern-level weight/risk-factor gradient; composition/fitness map; investigated behavioural delivery; investigated supplement shelf). They are not an efficacy ranking and not “A is best / F is worst.” Prefer the word **band** (or **role**) over **tier** in later drafts so the system is less likely to leak into a report-card reading.

**There is no A′ on this map.** Hypertension used a prime for a parallel hard-outcome product (salt substitutes). Type 2 diabetes correctly refused A′ for surgery. Obesity refuses it twice:

1. **SELECT is not A′.** It is internal to the semaglutide branch of Band A. Exporting it into a parallel band would invent a second medicines object and smear a high-risk CVD enrollment onto the whole map.  
2. **SOS is not A′.** It is observational. Treating it as a SELECT-class randomized warrant, or as a parallel hard-outcome sibling of medicines, is the exact error the procedures freeze forbids.

**There are not two remission routes.** Type 2 diabetes needed Bands B and C for surgery versus DiRECT-style structured remission. This campaign does not. Behavioural programmes are not DiRECT. VLCD is not a remission object. Do not print a “reversal” pair onto obesity because the sibling map had one.

Do not let later drafts, tables, or Phase 4 notes quietly convert letters into ranks. Do not print A–F on public pages.

### Placement pressure test

1. **Medicines remain Band A, not hub wallpaper and not “lifestyle first, shots later” folklore.** B1 is the comparative pharmacologic spine. SELECT does not demote the weight/harm seat, and the weight/harm seat does not upgrade every agent to MACE. The divider stays internal.  
2. **Procedures are Band B, not A′ and not a medicines footnote.** Long-term surgical weight seat. Endoscopy stays a distinct branch. Short-term incretin comparison annotates across A and B. SOS stays observational. This is why C3 was seated.  
3. **Eating patterns are Band C, not a named-diet championship and not DiRECT.** Six-month comparisons cannot travel without 12-month fade. VLCD stays internal. Vegetarian stays present without vegan transfer.  
4. **Exercise is Band D, not a second C and not an incretin rival.** Same broad lifestyle neighborhood as eating patterns, different jobs. Three jobs survive. Modest weight cannot erase lean mass and fitness, and cannot inflate into events.  
5. **Behavioural programmes are Band E, not C and not omitted.** No seated C. Pathway placement is not a GRADE spine. Delivery ≠ pattern quality. Regain and maintained difference stay inseparable. A topic page is honest investigation, not evidentiary parity with Bands A–D.  
6. **Popular supplements are Band F.** Investigated shelf for the desperate-reader test. Closed promotion gate. Search heat does not move F above A–B.

No frozen claim warrants a different placement.

## 3. Branching architecture derived from the evidence

### Finding

**One Obesity hub with six intervention topic pages is supported.** Broad clinical obesity as people search it is the spine. The frozen objects do **not** earn separate hubs by BMI class, pediatric vs adult, “lifestyle vs shots vs surgery,” or a merge with type 2 diabetes. Material differences appear as **hub annotations, topic self-identification where needed, and non-collapse of already-separate objects**, not as duplicate maps.

This is a Phase 3 finding, not a Step 0 assumption. Step 0 refused premature architecture and kept lifestyle, medicines, behavioural programmes, surgery, and popular weak claims in scope. Phase 1–2 then showed one pharmacologic seat with a weight-versus-MACE divider, one procedures object with two non-collapsed branches, three distinct lifestyle-adjacent decisions, and an investigated shelf. The architecture that follows is one map with mandatory teaching honesty, not BMI-split, pediatric-split, or diabetes-merged campaigns.

Six topic pages match six frozen objects. That count is earned from this campaign’s objects. It is not copied from type 2 diabetes’s six pages, which taught two remission routes this map does not have.

### Why one hub (and not BMI-class, pediatric, or diabetes hubs)

Keep one hub. Do **not** split into “overweight vs class I vs class II/III,” “adult vs child,” “shots vs surgery,” “AACE vs Obesity Canada vs NICE,” or “obesity vs type 2 diabetes.”

The stronger reason for one hub is:

1. **The reader enters through one broad decision problem** (“I have obesity, or I am searching for weight-loss options. What actually helps, and where does each option sit?”).  
2. **The frozen evidence supports one shared intervention landscape** whose main divergences are claim type (pharmacologic weight/harm plus one population-bound MACE warrant vs long-term procedural weight vs seated pattern/exercise syntheses vs investigated no-C objects), not six complete, independently calibrated treatment maps.  
3. **SELECT enrollment, A1–A3 culture disagreement, surgery vs endoscopy, and 6- vs 12-month fade are real and must be taught**, but as page-internal structure and hub annotation, not as duplicate hubs that would reprint the same six objects under different first-line stories.  
4. **We do not presently have enough independent objects to justify rebuilding multiple condition maps by BMI class, age, or diabetes status.** Pediatric evidence remains a watch. Type 2 diabetes remains a sibling campaign.

Entry problem shared. Controversies taught. Architecture uses annotations and page scope, not split hubs.

| **Behavioural programmes** | One topic object; investigated delivery | No seated C; regain *and* maintained difference; not eating patterns; meal-replacement component ≠ VLCD branch; no named-brand promotion |

### Why flattening into “lifestyle vs shots vs surgery” is not supported

A three-bucket public stack would erase endoscopy as a distinct branch, collapse programmes into “diet,” hide SELECT inside a kilogram league table or export it to every incretin, turn medicines into either a silent backdrop or a popularity contest, and leave the supplement shelf either omitted or over-promoted. The correct structure is one hub with a visible pharmacologic seat whose divider is taught, a procedures page that will not collapse, three uncollapsed lifestyle-adjacent pages, and an honest investigated shelf.

### Why eating patterns, behavioural programmes, and exercise stay separate topic pages

Already locked as separate objects. Phase 3 reaffirms: C1 is pattern quality with fade. Behavioural programmes are delivery with no seated C and a dual durability grain. C2 is three exercise jobs. Collapsing them into “lifestyle” would either invent programme-delivery evidence for Atkins/DASH, invent pattern-quality evidence for NHS packages, or let 2–3 kg erase lean mass and fitness.

### Why VLCD stays on the eating-patterns page

Already locked as a mandatory internal branch. Phase 3 reaffirms: about 4 kg extra at one year vs a behavioural programme alone is a formula-diet grain, not DiRECT, and not a seventh object. Meal replacement as a *programme component* stays on the behavioural page. Those two mentions must cross-link and must not swallow each other.

### Why surgery and endoscopy stay on one procedures page

Already locked as two named branches of one object. Phase 3 reaffirms: C3 is one network. Collapse would hide C3’s finding that endoscopic procedures except ESG were less effective than newer medicines. Split would invent a seventh object the freeze forbade. ESG’s exception is a named note, not an inheritance of long-term surgery.

### Why medicines stay one page (not a SELECT page, not a tirzepatide page, not a studio folio)

Object-index lock preserved. Weight ranking and MACE ranking are mandatory internal structure of one object. Splitting SELECT off would export a high-risk CVD trial onto a second map. Splitting tirzepatide off would turn kilograms into a separate destination. Investigational neighbors stay watches. Emerging Therapies remains the molecule-level room.

### Why this map is not type 2 diabetes

Overlap is real (incretins, surgery, eating patterns, exercise). Landscapes are not the same. SELECT lives here. SURPASS-CVOT and DiRECT live there. Dual agonists remain investigable here for the obesity decision. Do not reprint T2D’s two-remission architecture. Do not move SELECT onto the diabetes map. Do not absorb this hub into a cardiometabolic mega-page.

### Boundaries and watch items (not seats)

- Pediatric / adolescent obesity as this campaign’s map (A3 includes children; architecture does not pre-split)  
- Pregnancy and postpartum weight as this campaign’s map  
- Sleep as a major destination  
- Investigational incretins as approved-medicine objects (watches inside medicines)  
- Compounded peptides as a therapy branch (fence, not a protocol)  
- SURMOUNT-MMO and unfinished CVOTs as extra D seats

- Dedicated endoscopic library C separate from C3  
- Named single-supplement deep dives beyond the shelf  
- Type 2 diabetes merge, DiRECT import, or SURPASS-CVOT import  
- Emerging Therapies studio folios as this campaign’s objects  
- BMI-class or “overweight vs obesity” as separate hubs

### Public-page architecture supported at this stage

1. One obesity hub welcoming readers who search the condition or weight-loss options.  
2. Short orientation: medicines are on the map; kilograms are not heart attacks; SELECT does not cover everyone; surgery and endoscopy are different; programmes are not the same as named diets; supplements were investigated and not promoted.  
3. Six topic pages matching the six frozen objects.  
4. Hub must teach **weight ≠ events** and **weight ranking ≠ MACE ranking** as landscape principles before readers hit surrogate pages as if those settled heart outcomes.  
5. Eating patterns, behavioural programmes, and exercise are three pages. No combined “lifestyle” page. Procedures is one page with two branches, not a combined “shots vs surgery” page with medicines.  
6. Popular supplements earn honest investigated-shelf space, not promotion. Behavioural programmes earn honest delivery-object space, not a DiRECT import and not omission.  
7. Awareness color: locked August 13, 2026 to `#96720a` / `#684e08` (yellow → restrained mustard/gold as design identity, not an official ribbon; World Obesity Day blue unused because it crowds type 2 diabetes).
