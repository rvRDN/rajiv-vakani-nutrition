This is richer than a paper count. It maps quantity, quality, maturity, consistency, regulatory context, active trials, criticisms, and disagreement.

## Human non-RCT clinical reports

Small, uncontrolled, often same investigator cluster; tens of participants total across commonly cited pilots:

- Retrospective IA knee series (~16)  
- Intravesical interstitial cystitis pilot (n=12)  
- IV safety/PK pilot (n=2)  

These do **not** primarily test the forum claim (athlete soft-tissue / gut healing). UC Phase 2 claims in secondary blogs: verify-or-discard before elevating; do not bank on them.

## Reviews (evidence about evidence)

Sports-medicine systematic/scoping and narrative reviews repeatedly find preclinical majority vs minimal clinical inclusion. Translational/biopharmaceutics reviews describe stalled clinical development. These are high-value landscape documents.

---

## Preclinical + mechanistic

Large animal literature (tendon, ligament, muscle, wound, GI, vascular, etc.). Mechanistic themes: angiogenesis/VEGF-related, NO pathways, fibroblast migration, anti-inflammatory/cytoprotective framing.

**Landscape role:** Explains enthusiasm and plausibility. Does not establish clinical readiness.

Caveats: concentrated research traditions; possible positive-publication bias; historically, promising animal findings in multi-pathway cytoprotection have often translated less strongly in human medicine.

---

## Regulatory / anti-doping / product

- Not FDA approved  
- Compounding / Category history + 2026 PCAC/503A activity for BPC-157 salts. Jul 23, 2026: FDA staff recommended against Bulks List inclusion; PCAC voted 8–6–1 to recommend inclusion (advisory). As of Aug 2026, no final rule; still not lawful 503A compounding. Eligibility ≠ approval ≠ efficacy  
- WADA prohibited (practical for tested athletes)  
- Compounded/research-chemical purity and identity concerns appear in regulatory briefing narratives  

**Landscape weight:** Heavy. Shapes patient reality as much as rodent data.
