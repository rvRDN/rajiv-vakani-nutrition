**Narrative the landscape supports:**

MOTS-c is scientifically interesting and metabolically adjacent. The public conversation collapses three different objects: **endogenous MOTS-c levels**, **exogenous native peptide vials**, and a **discontinued synthetic analogue (CB4211)**. Preclinical work explains curiosity. It does not establish that gray-market injections improve insulin sensitivity or act as an “exercise mimetic” in people.

What this puts on the map that prior artifacts did not:

1. **Incretins / GH peptides / healing peptides:** nuclear-gene or synthetic therapeutic peptides with various evidence tiers.  
2. **MOTS-c:** a **mitochondrial-encoded** peptide story where most of the human “signal” people cite is either observational (endogenous) or about a **different molecule** (analogue).

---

## 1. Mechanism (landscape role)

Encoded in mitochondrial DNA; framed as a mitochondrial-derived peptide (MDP). Preclinical themes: AMPK-related signaling, insulin sensitivity, metabolic stress responses, exercise-capacity phenotypes in animals.

**Landscape meaning:** Explains why metabolism and aging conversations notice it. Mechanism does not equal a labeled drug or a finished human package.

---

## 2. Human evidence split (critical)

### A. Endogenous MOTS-c (observational)

Human studies correlating circulating MOTS-c with metabolic or exercise phenotypes. Useful biology. **Not** proof that injecting a vial reproduces those associations.

### B. Exogenous native MOTS-c

No published Phase 2/3 efficacy RCT package identified for native MOTS-c injections under obesity / insulin-resistance claims at freeze. FDA staff PCAC briefing materials (Jul 2026 cycle) treated human evidence for proposed compounding uses as insufficient.

**Registry caution:** ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT07505745 as a Phase 2a native MOTS-c prediabetes / overweight trial (Hudson Biotech). Same sponsor cluster previously appeared on a peptide NCT whose page labeled itself fictional/example. Treat NCT07505745 as **provisional registry chatter**; do not bank it as settled active-program fact until independently verified. Even if real, recruiting ≠ results.

### C. Analogue CB4211 (distinct object)

CohBar’s CB4211 is a **synthetic analogue**, not native MOTS-c. Phase 1a/1b work (including NCT03998514) explored safety / early signals in obesity / fatty-liver contexts; the development program did not produce an approved product. Citing CB4211 as “MOTS-c human trials” is an identity error.

## 3. Claim environment

What people claim:

- Exercise mimetic / mitochondrial optimization  
- Insulin sensitivity and fat loss from a peptide shot  
- Anti-aging / longevity biohacking staple  
- “Human data exist” (often meaning endogenous correlations or CB4211)

What the landscape supports:

- Strong preclinical metabolic story  
- Observational human biology around endogenous levels  
- Early analogue clinical work that is **not** native MOTS-c and did not become a drug  
- Thin published exogenous native efficacy package  
- Contested compounding / gray-market access  

---

## 4. Regulatory / access / sport

- Not FDA-approved  
- Jul 23, 2026 PCAC: FDA staff recommended against 503A Bulks List inclusion; committee voted 7–5–2 to recommend inclusion (advisory). As of Aug 2026, rulemaking unfinished; still not lawful 503A compounding  
- Often encountered via research-chemical / clinic channels rather than a labeled pharmacy product  
- WADA-prohibited framing reported (practical for tested athletes)

---

## 5. RD-ask / mission filter

Metabolic curiosity is real. Day-to-day RD volume is likely lower than BPC-157 or GH stacks. The notebook earns its place by teaching **object literacy** (endogenous vs exogenous vs analogue), not by matching tirzepatide length.

---

## 6. What an RD / clinician needs

1. Separate endogenous MOTS-c, injectable native MOTS-c, and CB4211.  
2. Do not treat AMPK slides as outcome proof.  
3. Nutrition and training still own insulin sensitivity and “exercise mimetic” goals.  
4. Product identity and sport-ban literacy if a patient already bought a vial.  
5. Keep expectations matched to a thin human exogenous package.
