About Me

Hi, I'm Rajiv! My journey into food and nutrition isn't just about science; it's about stories, culture, and the profound impact of what we eat on our lives and the planet.

I combine evidence-based nutritional science with a deep understanding of food systems, cultural heritage, and mindful living. Whether it's demystifying diet trends, exploring sustainable practices, or sharing plant-based insights rooted in Jain traditions, my aim is always clarity over dogma.

I believe that thoughtful eating is a cornerstone of a well-lived life, and my work is dedicated to helping you find joy, health, and purpose on your plate.
