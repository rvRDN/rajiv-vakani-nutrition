A Practical Guide to Vegetarian Protein

Guide June 8, 2026 Draft

A draft in progress. This page exists so the guide has a home in the Library while the piece is being written.

Most vegetarians are not protein deficient. Most also do not know where their protein actually comes from. This guide is for people who want a working answer rather than another internet thread. Daily targets, the sources that carry the real load, and how to compose meals without turning every plate into a calculation.

How much protein, actually

A working target range. Why the RDA is a floor, not a goal. How age, training, and recovery shift the number. How to translate the target into something you can think about at the kitchen rather than in grams.

The sources that actually carry the load

Dal, paneer, yogurt, eggs (if you eat them), tofu, tempeh, seitan, beans and chickpeas, lentils, milk, peas, soy milk. The approximate protein per serving, the practical caveats, and what to keep on hand.

How to compose a meal

A simple working pattern: one protein anchor, one grain or starch, one or two vegetables. Why this works without arithmetic. What to do at breakfast specifically, where vegetarian protein most often slips.

What I actually do

The handful of dishes and habits that quietly handle most of the target across a week. Not a meal plan. A working pattern.
