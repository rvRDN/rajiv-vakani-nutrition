Readingthe evidence

How nutrition studies, labels, and claims actually work; what they really say versus what they appear to say.

Most of what passes for nutrition advice begins with a study, a label, a headline, or a claim. The interesting work is figuring out what any of those actually say. Not what they appear to say. Not what someone summarizing them says. What they say once you read them carefully, in the context of everything else that has been said on the same question. The pieces here are about that work.
