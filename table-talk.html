What is Table Talk?

Table Talk is my bi-weekly newsletter where I dive deep into the intricate world of food. We explore everything from the origins of our ingredients and the complexities of global food supply chains to the latest in nutritional science and the cultural significance of what we eat.

My goal is to provide you with grounded insights, empowering you to make informed decisions that benefit your health, your community, and the planet. Expect thought-provoking articles, actionable advice, and a fresh perspective on everyday food wisdom.
