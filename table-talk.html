What is "Table Talk"?

"Table Talk" is a weekly newsletter for anyone seeking clarity in the often-confusing world of food. I blend evidence-based nutrition science with traditional wisdom to offer practical, sustainable approaches to well-being.

Each issue covers mindful eating, understanding cultural food practices, and demystifying complex nutritional information, all designed to empower you with knowledge and tools to make informed choices that truly serve your health and values.
