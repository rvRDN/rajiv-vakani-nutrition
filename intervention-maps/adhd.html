ADHD: what lifestyle and nutrition evidence actually supports

Behavior and skills training come first, and the best path is different for children and adults. Fix sleep when sleep is a problem. Exercise is worth doing. Nutrition matters, but it is not one single therapy. Some popular wellness products are not backed by good evidence.

This page maps the lifestyle and nutrition options. It does not rank the established first-line ADHD treatments, which are medication and structured behavioral or psychological therapy. This is not personal medical advice and not a plan to start or stop medication. Use it to have a better conversation with your clinician.

02

Worth doing

Sleep · Exercise

  • Nights are a mess. Does sleep care matter? Yes, when sleep is broken · melatonin is for sleep onset, not an ADHD cure
  • Does exercise actually help? Worth doing regularly · exact workout matters less than consistency
03

Nutrition

Many claims. Different strengths. Scan, then open one.

  • Is fish oil / omega-3 worth buying? Optional · small average effect · not decisive
  • Should we try a strict elimination diet? Only as a supervised, high-burden option · not routine first-line care
  • Do artificial food colors matter? Small effect · may matter more for some children
  • Will cutting sugar treat ADHD? No · not ADHD treatment
  • Do “healthy eating” patterns treat ADHD? Good for general health · thin as ADHD treatment proof
  • What about specialized multi-nutrient formulas? A few studied formulas show some benefit in certain studies · not the same as a drugstore multivitamin
  • What if iron or zinc is low? Treat documented deficiency as medical care · not an ADHD stack by itself
04

Maybe, with modest expectations

Optional. Not first-line.

  • Is mindfulness worth trying? Optional · modest · lower certainty · not first-line
05

Special cases

Narrow claims. Read the fine print.

  • What about prescription digital tools for attention? Narrow, test-specific attention claim · not a broad behavioral cure
06

Not where I’d spend first

Visible so you don’t have to wonder. Quiet on purpose.

  • Is neurofeedback worth a lot of money? The strongest trials do not support it as established ADHD treatment
  • Do “brain training” apps treat ADHD? They may train the game · not reliable ADHD treatment
  • Should we buy probiotics for ADHD? Interesting biology is not symptom proof · does not clear
