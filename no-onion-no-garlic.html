No Onion, No Garlic

Jain & Plant-Based Eating, Made Simple.
Nourishing traditions, modern plates.

Honoring Tradition. Eating with Awareness.

The No Onion, No Garlic Newsletter is for anyone navigating Jain, plant-based, or religious diets in today’s world. You’ll get grounded, real-world ideas that make this lifestyle easier, without losing its depth.

Each issue brings you recipes, nutrition tips, and food insights rooted in ahimsa, clarity, and intention. This isn’t about restriction. It’s about choosing food that supports your values, your health, and your life.

What to Expect in Each Issue

Recipes That Work

Easy meals made without onion or garlic. No guesswork, no fluff, just food that fits your diet and still tastes great.

Real-World Adaptations

Simple tweaks to make your favorite meals Jain- or plant-based without losing flavor.

Nutrition Without the Gaps

Guidance on how to meet your nutrient needs, even when you’re skipping common ingredients.

Food With Meaning

A closer look at the connection between food, ethics, and daily choices, without preaching.

Better Spice Blends

How to Eat Out (Without Compromising)

Tips for social events, restaurants, and travel when your food rules are non-negotiable.
