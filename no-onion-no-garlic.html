What is "No Onion, No Garlic"?

Beyond just avoiding specific ingredients, "No Onion, No Garlic" is a newsletter exploring Jain principles of food and compassion. It offers clarity-driven insight and plant-based recipes for those interested in a sattvic (pure, wholesome) lifestyle, whether you follow Jainism or simply seek a more mindful approach to food.

This newsletter unpacks the "why" behind these practices, connecting ancient wisdom with modern nutritional science and ethical considerations.
