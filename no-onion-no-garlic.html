Why No Onion, No Garlic?

This newsletter explores a compassionate and mindful approach to food, drawing inspiration from the principles of Jainism while integrating contemporary nutritional science. "No Onion, No Garlic" goes beyond just ingredients; it's about fostering a deeper connection with what we eat, respecting all life, and cultivating inner peace through our dietary choices.

Discover practical insights for plant-based living, traditional recipes adapted for modern kitchens, and reflections on how our food impacts our physical and spiritual well-being. Whether you follow a specific dietary path or are simply curious about mindful eating, this space offers clarity and guidance.
