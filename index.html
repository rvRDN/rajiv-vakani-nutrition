About Me

I’m Rajiv, and for over a decade, I’ve dedicated my life to exploring the fascinating intersection of food, culture, and well-being. My journey began in the vibrant kitchens of India, deepened through formal studies in Nutritional Sciences at the University of Florida, and expanded with professional experience at industry leaders like Kraft Heinz and The Coca-Cola Company.

Driven by a passion for understanding how we nourish ourselves—physically, culturally, and ethically—I’ve spent years researching, writing, and speaking about holistic approaches to food. From the ancient wisdom embedded in traditional diets to the cutting-edge insights of modern nutritional science, I believe true clarity comes from blending the best of both worlds.

My work aims to empower you with knowledge and practical tools to make informed, conscious food choices. Whether through my newsletters, articles, or consulting, I translate complex ideas into clear, actionable advice, helping you cultivate a healthier, more connected relationship with what’s on your plate.
