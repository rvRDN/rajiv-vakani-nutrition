My Approach to Nutrition

Hi! I'm Rajiv Vakani. I left a career in finance to pursue something that felt more human: helping people feel at home in their own bodies through nutrition. In finance, success was always being redefined, measured in quarters, not in meaning. I wanted to build something that would actually last.

I'm currently studying Nutrition, Dietetics & Food Studies at NYU, blending science, lived experience, and cultural insight into everything I do. I’ve trained in both food science and French culinary technique, and also the kind of traditional Indian cooking you experienced in your grandmother's kitchen.

This space is where I share what I'm learning, always with the goal of offering grounded, accessible knowledge. My work focuses on dispelling misinformation, simplifying complex dietary choices, and fostering a healthier, more intuitive relationship with food. I believe that thoughtful eating is a journey of self-discovery and cultural connection, not just a set of rules. I'm here to guide you with clarity and compassion.

My newsletters, Table Talk and No Onion, No Garlic, are extensions of this mission. Table Talk delves into broader topics of food systems and modern nutrition, while No Onion, No Garlic provides tailored insights and recipes for Jain and plant-based lifestyles.
