About Rajiv Vakani

I’m Rajiv Vakani—a food systems expert, nutrition consultant, and the mind behind "Table Talk" and "No Onion, No Garlic." My mission is to simplify complex topics around food, health, and conscious living, offering clarity and actionable insights.

With a background rooted in both traditional wisdom and scientific rigor, I help individuals and communities cultivate a deeper connection with their food choices, promoting well-being that extends beyond the plate. From sustainable practices to the nuances of plant-based nutrition, I share guidance that's practical, balanced, and deeply reflective.

My work is driven by the belief that informed choices lead to a more harmonious life. Whether you’re looking to refine your dietary habits, understand the impact of your food on the planet, or simply explore a more mindful approach to eating, you’ll find resources here to support your journey.
