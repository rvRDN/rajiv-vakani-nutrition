Where this came from.

I started reading because I wanted to solve a problem. At first it was my own health. The problem was eventually solved, but the reading didn’t stop. Somewhere along the way I realized I was no longer reading about myself. I was reading about ingredients, evidence, behavior, gardening, food systems, and why people eat differently around the world. The original question had become a much bigger one. That’s what I write about now.
