About Rajiv Vakani, RDN

Hi! I'm Rajiv Vakani. I left a career in finance to pursue something that felt more human: helping people feel at home in their own bodies through nutrition. In finance, success was always being redefined—measured in quarters, not in meaning. I wanted to build something more lasting.

I'm currently continuing my study in Nutrition, Dietetics & Food Studies at NYU, blending science, lived experience, and cultural insight into the work I do. I've trained in both food science and French culinary technique, as well as the kind of traditional Indian cooking you learn from standing beside your mom.

My goal is to make nutrition personal again. I won't hand you a one-size-fits-all plan. Instead, I'll offer real guidance that respects your lifestyle, your values, and your needs. Whether you're managing a health condition, following a plant-based or religious diet, or just trying to feel better in your own skin, I want to equip you with tools that make sense and actually feel doable.

From the garden to the gut microbiome, I'm here to translate the science into something you can actually use.
