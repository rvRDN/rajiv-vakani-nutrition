About Me

Hi, I’m Rajiv. For over a decade, I’ve delved into the intricacies of nutrition science, complemented by a lifelong immersion in traditional Indian food wisdom. My journey has led me to a simple truth: true nourishment extends beyond macros and calories. It's about how food connects us to our culture, our values, and ultimately, ourselves.

Through my newsletters, I distill complex topics into clear, actionable insights. Whether you’re navigating modern dietary trends or exploring ancient practices, my goal is to equip you with the knowledge and tools to make informed choices that resonate with your unique path to wellness.
