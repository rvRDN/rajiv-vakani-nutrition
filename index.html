About Me

I'm Rajiv Vakani—a nutrition student, Pathshala teacher, and curious human who thinks deeply about the food we eat and the lives we lead. I believe that what we choose to eat says something. Through my writing, I help others find clarity in nutrition, make intentional food choices, and honor both science and culture at the same table.
