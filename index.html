My Approach to Nutrition

Hi! I'm Rajiv Vakani. I left a career in finance to pursue something that felt more human: helping people feel at home in their own bodies through nutrition. In finance, success was always being redefined, measured in quarters, not in meaning. I wanted to build something that would actually last.

I'm currently studying Nutrition, Dietetics & Food Studies at NYU, blending science, lived experience, and cultural insight into everything I do. I’ve trained in both food science and French culinary technique, and also the kind of traditional Indian cooking you learn standing beside your mom.

My goal is to make nutrition personal again. I won’t hand you a generic plan. I’ll meet you where you are, with guidance that honors your lifestyle, your values, and your health goals. Whether you're managing a condition, following a plant-based or religious diet, or just trying to feel better in your own skin, I’ll help you build habits that make sense and actually feel doable.

From the garden to the gut microbiome, I’m here to translate the science into something you can live by.
