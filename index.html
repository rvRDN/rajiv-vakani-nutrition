About Me

Hi, I’m Rajiv. I help people find clarity in a noisy food world. I blend evidence-based nutrition science with traditional wisdom to offer practical, sustainable approaches to well-being.

My work focuses on mindful eating, understanding cultural food practices, and demystifying complex nutritional information. Whether through my newsletters or direct consultations, my goal is to empower you with knowledge and tools to make informed choices that truly serve your health and values.
